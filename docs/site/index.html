Your ARAM Mayhem augments, on stream.

It reads the augment you picked off the screen and stacks it on your broadcast. No account linking, no login, nothing to configure in the game.

Each pick joins the list

Four augments over a game, at levels 3, 7, 11 and 15. Silver, gold and prismatic are told apart by colour. The background is transparent, so it sits straight on the broadcast.

One small window beside OBS

It talks to OBS over websocket and serves the widget at a local address you paste into a browser source. Once it says connected, there is nothing left to press.

  • OSWindows 10 / 11 — 1920×1080 at 100% scaling
  • OBS Studio28 or newer
  • GameBorderless fullscreen recommended
  • InstallA single exe. Not even a .NET runtime
